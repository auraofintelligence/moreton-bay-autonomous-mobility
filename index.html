When does it become negligent not to use driverless transport?

If AI drivers keep getting safer and more reliable, that question is coming. The Bay Islands are a small version of the world to come, so this is a practical place to learn the systems, costs, benefits and compromises before the choice is made for us.

Learn it before it is imposed on us

The tough questions will not disappear. Facing them now gives locals, workers, visitors and operators a chance to understand the system and shape who benefits.

The goal: upgrade local passenger and delivery services towards useful 24/7/365 coverage, with existing operators included in ownership, work and profit.
