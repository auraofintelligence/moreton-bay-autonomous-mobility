Safer does not mean flawless

Leading driverless systems now report lower crash and injury rates per distance than human benchmarks inside defined operating areas. They still stall, make mistakes and need a clear response when something goes wrong.

AI-generated concept image. An accessibility and emergency-readiness review is shown.

What the strongest public comparison says

Waymo data through March 2026. Research checked 26 August 2026.

355 million km

driven without a human driver in Waymo's published comparison.

94% fewer

serious or fatal injury crashes than comparable human driving.

82% fewer

crashes causing any injury than comparable human driving.

That is strong evidence that Waymo is already safer than human drivers in the places and conditions measured. It does not mean no crashes, stalls or mistakes. It means the rate of harm is lower across a very large body of driving.
