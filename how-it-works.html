Two services, one useful network

Passenger vehicles and delivery aircraft do different jobs. They can share local hubs, booking support, charging, maintenance and a clear way to recover when something fails.

AI-generated concept image. An autonomous passenger vehicle and a separate delivery aircraft are shown.

How each service works

Passenger trip

  1. Book by app, phone or an assisted channel.
  2. The system matches a suitable vehicle and stop.
  3. The vehicle follows its approved route and operating limits.
  4. Support staff respond if the trip cannot continue normally.

Mainland-to-island air delivery

  1. An island resident, business or service orders food, medicine or an urgent repair part.
  2. Staff confirm the item, route, weather, collection point and approval.
  3. The aircraft carries it from a mainland depot across the bay.
  4. The recipient collects it at the agreed island point.

The shared support underneath

Booking and dispatch

One place to request, schedule and follow a trip or delivery.

Local hubs

Charging, storage, cleaning, maintenance and safe handover points.

Local jobs

Fleet operations, maintenance, customer support and emergency response.

Recovery

A practical response when weather, communications, a vehicle or a route is unavailable.
