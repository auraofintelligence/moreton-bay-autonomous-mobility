# Generated image record

All images were made with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 26 August 2026, then resized and compressed locally for the website. They are concept images, not photographs of an operating Moreton Bay service.

## Brand image

`brand/logo.png` and favicon crops: a simple square emblem combining a compact driverless community shuttle, a delivery drone and a curved Moreton Bay route, using navy, sea turquoise, coral and sand. Transparent background; no words, brands or watermark.

## Page heroes

- `heroes/home.webp`: a believable near-future shuttle and delivery drone on a coastal suburban avenue overlooking Moreton Bay at sunrise.
- `heroes/why-moreton-bay.webp`: a broad Queensland panorama linking hinterland, rail, town centres, coastline and mangroves.
- `heroes/global-trajectory.webp`: a cinematic near-future mobility operations landscape showing autonomous passenger fleets, charging hubs, a city skyline and delivery drones, with clear space for the page message.
- `heroes/how-it-works.webp`: an accessible low-floor driverless shuttle at a shaded local civic stop.
- `heroes/everyday-journeys.webp`: a winged delivery drone approaching a secure health-supplies landing cabinet beside the bay.
- `heroes/safety-and-trust.webp`: a human-led accessibility and safety review spanning an autonomous shuttle, delivery drone, operations desk and emergency-response exercise.
- `heroes/pilot-plan.webp`: an oblique regional view of multiple connected shuttle corridors, town centres, rail interchanges and drone delivery routes across Moreton Bay, with a planning team in the foreground.

## Shared direction

The prompt set used “civic documentary optimism”: credible Australian coastal places, ordinary people, warm subtropical light and near-term technology. It explicitly excluded readable text, brand marks, watermarks, cyberpunk styling, flying cars, drone swarms, military styling and impossible infrastructure.
